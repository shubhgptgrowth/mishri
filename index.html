Our story

It started with Maa’s steel dabba

Every Kolkata home has one: the dabba that comes out after dinner, the jar of jhalmuri masala by the stove, the golis tucked into a handbag for the train. Ours were always Maa’s.

Mishri bottles those recipes as she makes them: whole spices roasted on a slow flame, ground in small lots and mixed by hand, with no colour, no fillers and no shortcuts. Each jar is sealed so it reaches you the way it left her kitchen.

— From Maa’s kitchen in Baguiati, Kolkata

SOURCED WHOLE

Whole saunf, jeera, amla and almonds, never pre-ground.

SLOW-ROASTED

Roasted in small lots on a slow flame, so the oils stay fragrant.

BLENDED BY HAND

Ground and mixed to Maa’s ratios, tasted batch by batch.

SEALED FRESH

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you add colour or preservatives?

No added colour in any jar. Our mukhwas and masalas are made in small batches without preservatives, so please keep the lid tight and use a dry spoon.

How long does a jar stay fresh?

Each product page lists its shelf life: 6 to 12 months from packing, depending on the product. For the best aroma, finish an opened jar within 2–3 months.

Allergen information?

Shahi Badam Saunf and Shahi Almond Mix contain almonds (tree nuts). Amrit Goli and Cooling Chaas Masala use compounded hing, which contains wheat. Amrit Goli is not for children under 5.
