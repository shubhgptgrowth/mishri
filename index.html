Good to know

Any questions? We’ve got you.

Choosing the right after-meal blend matters. Here’s everything about our ingredients, freshness and delivery, kept as transparent as our jars.

Ask Maa on WhatsApp
Do you add colour or preservatives?

No added colour in any jar. Our mukhwas and masalas are made in small batches without preservatives, so please keep the lid tight and use a dry spoon.

How long does a jar stay fresh?

Each product page lists its shelf life: 6 to 12 months from packing, depending on the product. For the best aroma, finish an opened jar within 2–3 months.

How do I order?

Add jars or gift boxes to your basket and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. We’ll confirm availability, delivery and the total in the chat. You can also simply call or WhatsApp us.

Where do you deliver?

Send us your pincode on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm delivery options and charges for your area.

How do I pay?

We share payment details in the WhatsApp chat once your order is confirmed.

Do you do bulk or corporate gifting?

Yes. Message us the number of boxes, your preferred mix and the delivery date, and we’ll put together options for weddings, Diwali or corporate gifts.

Allergen information?

Shahi Badam Saunf and Shahi Almond Mix contain almonds (tree nuts). Cooling Chaas Masala uses compounded hing, which contains wheat.
